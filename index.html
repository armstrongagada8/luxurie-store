Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, Luxurie may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city/state, ZIP code, payment method selection, and the products you order. We collect only information needed to process and support your order.

2. How we use your information

We use order information to receive and confirm orders, communicate with customers, provide customer support, and maintain basic business records.

3. Order email service

When you submit an order, the order information is transmitted through our form/email service provider so the order can be delivered to Luxurie’s business inbox. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Payments

This website does not ask you to enter card numbers, CVV codes, PINs, or online banking passwords. If Luxurie later adds an online payment processor, payment information should be handled by that processor under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. Cookies and local storage

The storefront may use browser storage to remember your shopping cart during your visit. This site does not intentionally use browser storage to collect sensitive financial information.

6. Information sharing

Luxurie does not sell customer information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the store, process orders, communicate with customers, or comply with applicable law.

7. Data retention

Order information may be retained for customer service, accounting, fraud prevention, legal, or business-record purposes for as long as reasonably necessary.

8. Your choices

You may contact Luxurie to ask about personal information associated with an order or to request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to shopatluxurie@gmail.com.
